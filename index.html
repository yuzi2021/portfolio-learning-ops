Global classroom pilot

Connecting schools in India and Russia

I coordinated an international digital learning pilot that brought classes in India and Russia together. That meant aligning schedules across time zones, agreeing learning objectives and expectations with each school's educators, and keeping communication clear between people who had never worked together.

I ran the learning content on Claned, managing participants, materials and shared resources, and facilitated the live online sessions. After each session I collected feedback and built the improvements into the next one, covering the full cycle from planning and preparation to delivery, evaluation and follow-up.

GDPR guide & staff workshop

From stakeholder input to training that stays in use

Together with Laurea UAS, I gathered input from management, IT support, the Data Protection Officer and staff to find out what people actually needed. I then wrote a 57-page, legally reviewed guide and ran scenario-based workshops. The materials were adopted for annual staff development for 15–20 staff, and the research was published as a co-authored thesis.

HR ↔ LMS Reconciler

Making a routine LMS record check systematic

A browser-based tool that compares HR and LMS exports and flags records that need a closer look: missing accounts, leavers who still have access, permission mismatches and gaps in training rules. It then produces a report for follow-up. Learner data is read in the browser and never uploaded.
